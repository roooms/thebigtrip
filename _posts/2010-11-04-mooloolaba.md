---
title: "Mooloolaba"
date: 2010-11-04 00:34:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
We were picked up from the bus stop by two staff from the backpackers in their shuttle bus. On the way we got filled in on what to see and do in Mooloolaba. Arriving at the hostel there was a great vibe and we felt immediately at home. We went for a walk before sun down and discovered just how beautiful the area was; Mooloolaba sits on the Mooloolah River and the area is like one huge marina, along with a great beach facing out in to the South Pacific Ocean. We had booked in to eat curry at the backpackers as they cook up a massive meal each evening so people can eat on the cheap. We made it back around dusk and sat around a big table tucking in.

The next day was spent almost entirely at the beach. We got down there quite early and just chilled out, reading our books and enjoying the sunshine. Nat spotted a grey cloud moving in towards late afternoon so we packed up and made our way back, getting caught in a shower just fifty yards from the hostel. It had been a nice day though and we cooked up a stir fry (avoiding the Domino's pizza on offer) before having a quiet one at the hostel.

Day three greeted us with some cloud but it was warm enough to take a walk along the beach towards Alexandra Headland were we stumbled across the Australian War Dog Memorial, dedicated to the tracker dogs used in the Vietnam War. For any dog lovers it had a tear-jerker of an inscription on it:

*Down jungle tracks, through shot and shell,*

*Ears pricked, keen sense of smell;*

*Our tracker dogs with care and poise,*

*Alert to ambush, foreign noise;*

*Never a whimper, whine or bark,*

*Their service honoured with this plaque;*

*No medals pinned to hairy chest,*

*They stayed behind, they were the best.*

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/11/17049972-War_Dog_Memorial-3231-90372.jpg)

When we got back to the hostel we booked tickets to go to Australia Zoo the following day and witnessed the build up for Goon Pong ([Beer Pong](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beer_pong) with cheap wine) night. We had missed out on entering the competition, but got to enjoy watching all the girls dressed as footballers and the guys dressed as WAGs whilst playing a knockout tournament to win prizes. It was also one of the guys 25th birthday and as he had been at the backpackers for ages there was cake and presents being handed round. It was a really nice atmosphere and a great night to finish our stay in Mooloolaba.

The next morning we checked out and made our way to the Australia Zoo Bus pick-up point, just around the corner. Little did we know that it would be the last time we saw our phone for two weeks!
