---
title: "Crescent City"
date: 2010-04-15 23:17:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
Sitting on the north coast of California, Crescent City was around a 5 hour drive from our last stop, Crater Lake. We drove the amazing Highway 199 through state parks and national forests, until a small sign that read "Oregon Thanks You, Come Back Soon" let us know we were leaving Oregon. Around the next bend was a much bigger sign welcoming us to California!

Driving through Six Rivers National Forest we saw our first big collections of redwoods, as the road followed a river toward the coast.

Arriving in Crescent City we were reminded just how small Shady Cove was. We were surrounded by motels, fast food and petrol stations again. We drove down to the bay and found the Northcoast Marine Mammal Center, where there were a few poorly seals in pens outside. We chatted to the staff for a while and decided to come back tomorrow as it was about to close.

We checked out the price for a room at Econolodge and didn't need to search any further. We finished the night with a good meal at a local fish restaurant that was recommended to us.

The next morning we grabbed breakfast at the motel before heading out to Pebble Beach Drive just around the corner from the main bay. We checked out the lighthouse, but it was shut due to the tides preventing anyone getting over to it. We stopped again at the Northcoast Marine Mammal Center and got chance to learn a lot more about what they do. We made a small donation and decided to hit the road for the next stop, Eureka.
