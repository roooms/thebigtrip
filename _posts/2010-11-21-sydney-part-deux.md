Our bus journey went fairly quickly and we both managed to get some sleep, especially Dan after his all day sesh!

We drove over the Harbour Bridge and arrived in a cloudy Sydney.

Made our way over to Kings Cross and settled back into Sydney life by going out with Sammy, Giles, Fiona and some of her friends from Uni for a fabulous Thai meal accompanied of course by some vino.

The next day was thankfully sunny so we got sorted and went to Maroubra beach, this was a great place to spend a few hours and when the sun started to dip behind some clouds we headed home to prepare ourselves for our mini Halloween party. Sammy and I prepared some food then we got into our budget but effective costumes just in time for Daniel and Stuart arriving. After devouring our food we decided to share some of our drinking games that we had picked up along our travels. Feeling suitably drunk we then played the chocolate game, no need for dessert!

On Sunday we ended our Halloween weekend by having a wander around a nine hole golf course in Bondi, occasionally taking a couple of swings then refuelling at Sabbaba which does amazing Lebanese food, yum!

The next two days were a bit of a right off, Sydney had rain, rain and more, yep you’ve guessed it, rain! So we spent our days not doing a lot and our evenings chilling with Sammy and Giles and eating the kind of food made for rainy nights, Malaysian and Thai takeaways.

Finally on Wednesday we got a bit of reprieve and had a sunny day which we spent in the city and at Darling Harbour. We checked out the Christmas displays up in all the shop windows which does look a bit strange when you are outside in the hot sun.

Had another rainy one on Thursday but Friday was better again, we took a walk to Mrs Macquarie's Point in the botanical gardens to watch the Red Bull ramp being put up for the Flugtag on Sunday.

That evening we went to Fi’s for dinner and a film, lovely to see Fi’s pad as the last time we were there we had just helped her to move in!

Saturday was another overcast one which we tried to make the most of by going to Watsons Bay for fish and chips but as we arrived the heavens opened and the food place only took cash so we took it as a sign and headed home via a little place which does great fish burgers!

That evening me and Dan headed into the city to meet up with Caitriona and Laura from our west coast tour, and some of Laura’s friends from home. They had already been on the goon so were a bit ahead of us. We did a bit of catching up whilst listening to a really good band before hopping into taxi’s and going to Kings Cross.

We all spent about an hour trying to get into various bars but due to Tom wearing shorts and Shane being slightly too intoxicated it was a fail. We left them to it and decided to call it a night.

Sunday started off a bit cloudy but soon turned into a glorious day which we were kind of happy with apart from the fact we were all wearing jeans and none of us had sun cream on! Nevertheless we had a great day at the Flugtag watching random flying machines generally drop off the end of a ramp. There were a few who made it a little way out but these were all dwarfed when the world class hang-glider pilot took to the ramp to see how far he could go. It was amazing, he flew just past the world record Flugtag marker. Amazing to see someone go that distance although it obviously lacked the comedy value of the others. We were also compelled to go home and google the world record holder as none of the competitors came close!

After a refreshing cider Me and Fi cooked a roast dinner for us all. A very tasty end to a great weekend.

The next week was still a bit of a mixed bag as far as the weather was concerned and some of the days were kind of quiet. Monday we made it to see Sculptures by the Sea which is where art is installed along the Bondi to Coogee walk. We made it to Tamarama where the art finished and carried on to Coogee to meet Caitriona for a coffee before the heavens opened and the thunder storm which had been threatening us finally hit Luckily by the time we were heading home it had stopped.

Wednesday I went to meet up with one of my clients from back home, Hannah, who has been travelling for a few months. Had a great evening catching up and she got me really exited about Asia. Our couple of drinks turned into a whole evening out and we ended up in Wagamama’s!

Thursday was a nice day and me and Dan headed down to Elizabeth Bay for lunch to enjoy some sun. That evening we met Fi in the city for a quick snack then we were off to the cinema, from there we went home with Fi to spend a few days at her place.

The next day we had a shopping list of BBQ supplies to pick up which was easily sorted then we had a wander around Paddington in the sun.

We went for some food in a place called ‘a fish called paddo’ courtesy of Ben. We ate at the Coogee branch with Ben and Em when we were here four years ago and Ben donated some cash for us to spend on crayfish back when we were in NZ. Unfortunately we had already passed the crayfish place and just hadn’t found anywhere afterwards so we decided to eat there and we weren’t disappointed. I went for tuna steak marinated in garlic and chilli and Dan had king fish marinated in basil, Mmmm Mmmm. Cheers Ben. After our food we met Fi in the pub next door for a few then we went down to Darling Harbour to meet up with Caitriona and Vanessa, another girl from our tour. Had a few drinks out then walked back to Fi’s which is quite a way. When we got back Fi was still up and we played a few games of drunken scatch and badminton before bed.

After a week of speculation Saturdays weather was lovely and we got a lift to Freshwater beach with Sammy and Giles, had a few hours of lazing and an awakening dip in the sea before going shopping for Fi’s first bbq!

Back at Fi’s we got on with prepping the food and got it all ready just as everyone arrived, Jo and James, Sammy and Giles, Daniel and Stuart and Mike and Leanne all arrived at about the same time The boys were in charge of the bbq while we were griddling the veggie fare in the kitchen, we ended up with enough food for about three times the amount of people who were there but it was all good.

After attempting to devour the food we had more drinks and played ring of fire followed by the longest game of celebrity heads we have ever played!

All in all Fi’s first bbq was a great success!

Sunday was also lovely and after a joint mammoth clean up (we managed to use nearly all the contents of Fi’s kitchen) me, Fi and Dan went to Rushcutters Bay for a picnic to use up the leftovers then had a walk to try to burn off some of the food.

That evening we went back to Sammy and Giles’s place.

Monday was our final day of prepping and packing so of course it was raining as we had to be in town for a bit. Managed to get all the stuff we needed without getting too wet and eased it by meeting Caitriona for a coffee and checking out one of the biggest xmas trees I have ever seen, it was in the Queen Vic Building and was three storeys high! We also met Kayla (our friend from Byron Bay) for a cider at rainy Darling Harbour.

That evening we went out for Japanese with Sammy, Giles, Daniel, Stuart and Fi, got through four bottles of wine then ended up going to a bar with Sam and Giles to meet Laura and her mates. Our quiet evening with a couple of drinks turned into a big night out, we got in at 3am after Dan got a pic with a cop sporting a similar facial addition, not sure if it was for Movember! And I think we got into bed at about 5am!

Needless to say Tuesday morning was a struggle which was bad as we had most of our packing to do and a flight to catch, luckily we didn’t have to get our shuttle bus to the airport till 11.45am

Got to the airport fine and joined the massive check in queue which we spent an hour stood in, finally arrived at the check in desk only to be told that as we didn’t have printed proof of our onward travel out of Singapore we couldn’t be checked onto the flight! Dan then had 9 minutes to go through security to the internet café and print out our Air Asia flights, as most of you know this didn’t happen and he arrived back just as the girl on desk no 35 closed the flight even though she knew he was on his way! Surprisingly I kept my calm and we spoke to a nice guy called Walter on the customer service desk to book us onto the next flight to Singapore which ended up being the same time the next day.

We went and booked ourselves a return shuttle and headed back to Sammy’s, luckily she had gone home early due to a strange, sudden illness (hangover) and was able to let us in. We then spent the afternoon re-booking our flights to Bangkok and Chiang Mai as it was too late to change them, printing out our new flight details and sending an email to Jetstar to see if we could get some compensation.

Had a comforting fish pie and a chilled out evening.

**Groundhog Day.**

Had a much better start to the day and got to the airport even earlier, check in was a breeze and had loads of time to grab breakfast. Our first flight of the day to Darwin was a bit late taking off so we arrived late but still had time for some lunch. We arrived in Singapore and were planning on spending the night in one of the rest areas but we hadn’t thought about the fact that we wouldn’t be able to check in that early for our flight in the morning. We ended up booking a cheap hotel in the red light district and getting the MRT out there. It wasn’t too far out and we found a great little Indian place where I had the biggest dosa type roll I have ever seen, it was about two foot long! I wish I had taken the camera.

We settled into our tiny room for a good nights sleep.

Our second day of two flights went well, breakfast in Singapore and a slightly delayed flight to Bangkok where we joined the biggest queue I have seen in an airport which was for our Visa’s. We collected our bags walked through customs then straight round to departures where we checked in and had lunch in Bangkok airport overlooking some nice gardens.

Our flight to Chiang Mai also took off late but the Pilot managed to make up the time and we landed at 5pm, the view from the plane was green and hilly and we were excited to take our first proper steps in Thailand and have dinner in Chiang Mai!
