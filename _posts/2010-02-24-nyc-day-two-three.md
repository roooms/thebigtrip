---
title: "NYC day two & three"
date: 2010-02-24 21:36:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
Yesterday was nearly a wash out thanks to non-stop rain from the minute we left the hotel, to arriving back in the evening. We persevered and queued for amazing pancakes at [Clinton Street Baking Co](http://www.clintonstreetbaking.com/), followed by a walk through the Lower East Side to see the [Manhattan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Bridge) and [Brooklyn Bridge](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Bridge). We continued down to Battery Park to see the outline of the Statue of Liberty through thick fog, before heading up Broadway for a quick look around Century 21 and then Ground Zero. Soaked through we jumped on the subway and headed back to the hotel, only venturing out once more (still in the rain) to Cafe 28 for an evening snack.

Day three was much more successful (dry) and we got to take some pictures on our journey around Manhattan Island. We went to Mercer Kitchen for breakfast and had a wander around [SoHo](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoHo) for an hour or so before heading over to West Village and 66 Perry Street for [some reason most of Nat's friends will understand](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrie_Bradshaw). After that we caught the subway up to 54th street between 5th and 6th avenue to find a very small shoe shop by a guy named Manolo. It looked a bit pricey so we didn't stay long and made our way to Central Park to escape the crowds. Day three ended with a nice couple of drinks and a hamburger at [P.J.Clarke's](http://pjclarkes.com/pjclarkes/), which had a fantastic atmosphere and great staff.

We have just checked in on-line for our flights to Seattle tomorrow, and are looking forward to meeting up with some friends and checking out the freshies to be had, following the [snow due on the West Coast](http://www.catchpowder.com/snow.go?zipcode=98122) over the next day or so.
