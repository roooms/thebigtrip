Hi family and friends. I'm very bad at writing online diary style blogs, but we decided early on to create a separate site, where we can keep everything related to our trip, so those interested in our travels can keep up and keep in touch easily. Facebook may well get used for short status updates, but we'd like to keep the majority of it here so those without Facebook can follow us and it's easy for us to look back on as well (already thinking about what happens after the year out, and we haven't even left yet!). So from here on in we will be posting updates, pictures and videos to this site, so even the most technophobic of family members can see what's happening on our journey.
