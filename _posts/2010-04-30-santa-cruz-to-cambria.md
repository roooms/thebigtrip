---
title: "Santa Cruz to Cambria"
date: 2010-04-30 00:49:00
author: "Natalie"
---
We saw lots more fantastic scenery all along the route to Santa Cruz.

We arrived and headed straight to the famous boardwalk which houses the oldest beach-front amusement park on the west coast.

Had a walk along and it was eerily quiet, I guess it gets busier on the weekends and in the summer.

We headed off to find a room for the night and spent the evening planning, still haven't booked any accommodation for the Cooks but have some ideas, not long to go now till we leave the USA. I'm quite excited about the next chapter!

The next day we left early and headed off to do the Big Sur drive, supposedly the best coastal drive you can do.

We stopped so many times I can't remember but the pictures were worth it. A few places in the pics are, Bixby Bridge, which is a very popular bridge to photograph. Pfeiffer Beach, which we stopped at for a well earned break. It was really windy and the waves were crashing through holes in the rock formations, it also had swirls of purple sand all over the beach.

Then there was Piedras Blancas which is a favorite hangout of a huge Elephant Seal colony, I was obviously in my element watching them all lay around and occasionally go for a swim, have a scratch or get into an argument but eventually the smell got the better of us and we carried on a bit further to our decided destination of the night, Cambria.

We had already read up about a hostel there and it is part of an organisation called Hosteling International which we are part members of so went there and earned a stamp towards our full membership. The house was really cute and about 100 years old. Everything was mismatched and very vintage looking with wonky floorboards.

Headed out for wander, which didn't take long, and ended up at a restaurant called Robins which had been recommended to us by the book and the hostel. Had some fab food (pictures are included, especially for Ben) and really good service. All in all a nice end to a tiring driving day.
