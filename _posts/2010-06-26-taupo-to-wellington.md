---
title: "Taupo to Wellington"
date: 2010-06-26 01:35:00
author: "Natalie"
---
Arriving in Taupo was a little bit of a let down. We drove straight to the Lake that it is famous for which is actually the crater of a Volcano and is the size of Singapore! We parked up to have a look at it whilst being lashed by rain and I have to say it didn't look it's best under the grey sky.

We drove around a bit hoping to find somewhere to free camp but it seemed the places available were closed for the winter so we headed to a regular site which we were quite pleased about as it ended up being a chilly night.

Because of this I invented a nose warmer out of a bit of foam roll mat, it worked quite well and I don't think it looked too stupid?! (Picture included)

The next day started off better with some clear skies but unfortunately we spent most of our time in Taupo at a garage trying to get our drinking water pump fixed. All we managed to find out after about three hours of waiting around was that the pump was broken and that they couldn't get us a new one till the next day! They did manage to order us a part to be delivered the next day in Napier as that was our next destination.

To get us ready for our drive to Napier we went to try out Burger Fuel which is a chain of resturants offering a healthier burger different options to the others. Our burgers were called Bio-fuel and they were really nice they have Aioli instead of Ketchup, ours also had a free-range fried egg and beetroot (it's the Kiwi way and it's good) as well as the usual burger requirements. We also opted for Kumara fries which are also known as yam-frites or sweet potato fries, we took a liking to them in Whistler so was good to taste them again Another brilliant discovery we made whilst in Burger Fuel were their patent protected 'Doofers', these are brilliant little cardboard holders that your burger perfectly fits in just enough to hold it and stop everything falling out the bottom, Legend!

Someone should bring them to the UK.

The route to Napier was fab, the weather cleared as we drove towards the coast and we got great views of some snow capped mountains. Once we arrived we had a quick look round while there was still some daylight. Napier is one of the most complete Art Deco cities in the world and so it was lovely to have a quick look around and see some of the fantastic buildings. The streets were lined with palm trees and globe street lights and even had Art Deco styled drain covers!

Our first stop the next day was the garage to check if the pump had arrived, it hadn't but was apparently on the way. We went off to have a proper look around the city and did a bit of a walking tour around all the cool buildings. We stopped off in an Art Deco shop where they were showing a free film about how Napier became so Deco. There was a huge earthquake in 1931 which flattened the city and also lifted some land from the ocean floor, Napier started to be rebuilt and the architects used the fashionable style of the time which was Art Deco. Every February the city gets together to embrace the Deco and they have a whole weekend of getting dressed up and partying Art Deco style.

After our look around the City we went to the Aquarium, mainly to see a Kiwi. There were the usual selection of fish but also some we hadn't seen before including some blind fish who have no eyes!

Then we came to the darkend room of the Kiwi, at first you couldn't see anything but then rustling out of the bushes came two Kiwi to eat some food. They were bigger than I thought they would be but still very cute and hillarious when they walked.

We walked through a tunnel in the Oceanarium and watched a scuba diver feeding the big Fish, Stingray's and Sharks. It was only her second dive with them and was understandbly nervous, epecially when the Sharks decide to get closer to see what is left in the bucket!

After a pretty cool morning we headed back to the garage to see if they had any news. The woman in there, Roz was fuming as we walked in and had just come off the phone. Our pump was still in Auckland and so wouldn't be here till the next morning.

So another night in Napier, not a bad place to have to stay another night.

Our last half day in Napier consisted of getting the van fixed and just having another mooch around the city, it was a really nice warm day so we were glad to be out and about before being sat in the car for five and a half hours to Wellington.

We managed to book our ferry tickets to the South Island too so that is exciting.

So our journey to Wellington wasn't too bad, arrived late so just found a site and stayed there. The next day we were up early and off into Windy Welly. It's a really nice modern city full of cafe's, bar's and resturants. We took advantage of the good weather and went up to a look out over the city via a cable car, fab views out across the harbour. Then we walked back down to the city through the Botanical Gardens which were nice but I'm sure they look better in the Summer.

That afternoon we took advantage of the Te Papa museum being open till nine pm. This was the best museum we have been to and I would go as far as saying I wouldn't have gone to the others if I had known how good this one was going to be. It is also free to get in which is always nice but you can give a donation.

The museum is huge and has lots on offer, we spent nearly four hours there and didn't see it all. The displays are fab too, lots of them were interactive and many used high tech computer wizzardry. We saw a collection of short films about people living in NZ, experienced an earthquake aftershock in a specially made house and went into a beautiful Marae (traditional Maori meeting house). There was a little bit of everything so you didn't get bogged down by one subject. That evening there was also a free show going on by the Seven Brothers, we managed to catch a bit of The Laughing Samoan's act which was really funny

The next day started off a bit wet so we chilled out in the van for a bit and waited it out. I'm glad we did as when we did venture out the sky cleared and we had a fairly good day. Did a bit more wandering aound the city then headed back to camp for a reasonable nights sleep ready for our early wake up call to get to the ferry for 7am!
