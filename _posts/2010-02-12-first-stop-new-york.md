10 days to go and we have finally booked our hotel for the first three nights of our trip. Having spoken with many friends who have all been to New York, we have a lot of great advice on what are must do's and what can be dropped from the NYC itinerary.

Booking the hotel with only 10 days to go may have been a bit late, and we settled on the [Carlton Hotel on Madison Avenue](http://www.carltonhotelny.com/). None of our friends have stayed there (to our knowledge) but after looking around, all recommendations were above our self-imposed £100 per night budget.
