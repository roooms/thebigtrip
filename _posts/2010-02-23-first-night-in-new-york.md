We arrived at our hotel around 5pm last night so had chance to unpack a bit of our stuff before heading out to see some sites. We had a rough plan on where we wanted to go for drinks ([View Lounge](https://maps.google.co.uk/places/us/manhattan/ny/new-york/broadway/1535/-view-lounge?hl=en)) but we were really hungry and advised the buffet there was pricey and nothing to write a blog about. So we did what everyone who is about to spend $30 on two drinks should do when they are hungry, we had [Wendy's](http://www.wendys.com/).

After accidentally ordering 3 burgers instead of 2, and a **medium** Dr Pepper that was almost too heavy to carry, we had the energy to spend an hour trying to find the View Lounge, a rotating restaurant on the 48th floor of the Marriot Marquis Hotel in Time Square.

On our wanderings we saw the Empire State and Flatiron, before giving in and asking where we should be heading to find the Marriot Marquis. Opposite direction? Oh right, we should probably get going then. Once we were on the right track we could see Times Square about 10 blocks away, and the nearer we got the more it was like walking back in to daylight.

At the top the view was great and we got an edge seat so we could look out as the whole restaurant slowly turned, giving 360 degree views of Manhattan. I tried the Brooklyn Beer and Nat had a Cosmo (that sadly couldn't come close to those she had in Kenilworth).

An hour of slowly rotating later and we'd seen it all and headed back to the hotel, picking up some bottled water on the way to avoid the $10 a litre stuff in the mini bar.
