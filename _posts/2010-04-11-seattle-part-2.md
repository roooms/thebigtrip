We only just made it on to the train to Seattle. Cutting it a bit fine leaving the hotel, we arrived at the train station having forgotten that all the US/Canada border stuff (fingerprints, mugshots, bag checks) is done before we board the train this time. The staff were really helpful though, I was half expecting them to say we had to wait 12 hours until the next one, but we made it to our seats just as we were scheduled to depart.

The journey back along the coast was no less spectacular than the one we had on the way to Vancouver just over a month before, but it was 6.40am so it wasn't long before I was asleep and only waking as we stopped along the way at each station. Arriving back in Seattle we checked our bags in to storage at the train station and went for a walk along the waterfront. We had around 90 minutes until our friend Ron could meet us at his apartment, that he was kindly moving out of for as long as we needed so we could stay for free! It was having friends in Seattle that made it feel so much like home; that and the weather anyway.

The plan was to stay for a few days, work out exactly how we were going to get to LA for May 2nd, and what we were going to see along the way.

Once unpacked we headed out to [Snowqualmie Falls](http://www.snoqualmiefalls.com/) with Ron and Cheryl for a few hours, before heading to a [local cafe](http://www.the5pointcafe.com/About.php) for a bite and settling in for the night reading Lonely Planet's.

Over the next five days we ate at some great, cheap restaurants and drank at a few of the thousands of coffee shops in Seattle. The route south changed many times. We were gutted to learn that during April many of the entrances and trails in Yellowstone are closed while the area is cleared of winter snow, ready for the summer season. The park is still open, but only a fraction of it, not including the big attractions (geysers, hot springs etc) and therefore possibly not worth the eleven hour drive east to see it. We decided to stick to the coast and see some of Oregon on the way down to California.

In the middle of this feverish planning we joined Jon, Jenna and their friends for a pub quiz at a fantastic ale house called [Attic](http://www.atticalehouse.com/). We won a free pitcher during the quiz, and ended up in a four way tie for the win after all the points had been totaled. We sealed the win by one of our group downing a beer and knowing where Dorothy was heading in The Wizard Of Oz.

On our penultimate day in Seattle we went on the [Underground Tour](http://www.undergroundtour.com/), and learned about the [pre-1889 Seattle](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Underground) before [fire destroyed](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Seattle_fire) a large part of the city.

The following day, with at least the first few stops of our route decided, we got a great deal on car rental and headed for [Cannon Beach](http://www.cannonbeach.org/) along Route 101.
