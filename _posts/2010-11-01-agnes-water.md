We arrived in Agnes Water early after our night bus journey. The hostel was only up the road from the bus stop but we took the free shuttle there anyway.

It was a really sunny day and the hostel rented out cheap push bikes so we took a couple out and rode down to the shops to pick up some supplies. Back at the hostel we dumped the bikes and walked 3 minutes to the beach, soaked up some afternoon sun then went to the hostel to get the bikes.

We rode up the road to a residential street called Discovery Lane where we saw a Kangaroo with her Joey, they watched us watching them for a while before bouncing away.

Next we carried on to a lookout to watch the sunset then headed back before it got too dark.

Our second day in Agnes was pretty chilled, we were supposed to go surfing but got up too late and so did lots of planning which we rewarded with a couple of drinks in the evening. Got an early night ready for our 6 am start.

Our bus journey to Mooloolaba was good, the weather was lovely and we passed by some really nice destinations such as Rainbow Beach and Noosa but as we arrived in Mooloolaba were glad with our choice of destination.
