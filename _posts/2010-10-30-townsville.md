Our bus journey was pretty good although the weather as we drove down the coast was not. We passed through a place called Tully which confirmed it’s reputation for being the wettest place in Australia. We watched a film on the bus and chatted to Pete which made the journey easier and soon we arrived in Townsville.

It was just a short walk to our hostel where we got checked in and chatted to our roomies. Had a little wander down the main street and found some cheap food and a couple of beers to finish our day.

The next day was pretty overcast so we took a walk down the beachfront called The Strand. Townsville has a man made lagoon due to the stingers in the water but unfortunately it was being cleaned so we couldn’t take a dip.

We decided to go to Reef HQ which is an Aquarium, it was small but really good and we saw a Sawfish for the first time. Also visited the Turtle hospital which had a few patients in suffering from emphysema, this causes them to float on the surface of the sea which leaves them open to danger but also means they are spotted and can be rescued and helped. Most of the Turtles recover from this illness well.

After our interesting afternoon we got food and chilled out in the hostel as it was raining, booked our next bus journey for the following day.
