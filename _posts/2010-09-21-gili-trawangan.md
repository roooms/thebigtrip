I was a little bit apprehensive about our boat trip to Gili Trawangan, firstly I had been suffering with ‘Bali belly’ for the last couple of days and secondly, I’m not that great on boats!

The journey took about an hour and a half longer than we had thought but it was a pretty good trip with no seasickness, yey!

We got a good view of all three of the Gili’s as we had to do a quick stop at Lombok, they looked idyllic and we were glad we had chosen to go there rather than the much bigger island of Lombok.

The boat docked a little way from the beach so we got our feet wet as we walked onto the beach.

Gili T is the busiest of the three islands but still doesn’t have any motorised traffic, the only way to get around is by walking, push bike or cidomo which is a horse drawn cart. Very cute although you don’t want to get in their way!

The island has a really relaxed vibe and there was a good mix of people there, some to party and some to chill out.

We didn't have to wander around for long before we found some accommodation for the next five nights, pretty basic but not bad considering our budget.

We spent the afternoon on the beach which was lovely and white and getting in the sea was just like getting into a bath.

That evening we had left it a bit late for food so we had to settle with snacks from one of the little shops, when we got back to the room we were faced with the challenge of retrieving a cockroach out of the toilet, believe me I tried to flush the little bugger but he could swim! Then Dan spotted a rather large spider on the wall right next to the toilet so he managed to collect him up in a bucket and set him free.

Finally we were able to use the loo and go to bed!

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/09/15284038-IMG_5231.JPG)

The next day we slept in so had a lunch rather than breakfast and due to a dodgy tummy and a bit of heat stroke had a really lazy day. In the afternoon we took a walk to the South West corner of the island to watch the sunset and we got to see the huge Volcano on Bali, Gunung Agung which is usually covered with cloud.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/09/15284046-P1060465.JPG)

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/09/15284047-P1060476.JPG)

We spent the next couple of days getting up late, having brunch and finding different bits of beach to sit on with all important shade. I think this is one of the hottest places I have been to and found myself having to find shade when I would normally be laying out in the sun all day long.

In the evenings we found some local street food vendors and sat in some of the beach front bars soaking up the atmosphere.

Our last full day on Gili T was definitely the hottest while we were there, I spent most of the day in the sea but by the afternoon it was cooler. We went for a snorkel off the beach and there were quite a lot of fish to be seen.

That evening we treated ourselves to a nice meal at a place called Scallywags, I had a really tasty Snapper in a Pineapple yellow curry and Dan had a juicy home made cheese burger washed down with a good ole Bintang.

Afterwards we sampled a delicious chocolate brownie to share and then walked down to an outdoor cinema where we relaxed on futons on the sand to watch ‘Repo Men’.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/09/15284056-IMG_5281.JPG)

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/09/15284042-P1060434.JPG)

Leaving Gili T was not quite so smooth as our arrival, the boat turned up late and then because of the tides it couldn’t get close to the beach so we had to be ferried across on a smaller boat. This would have been ok but it took them an age to get the second lot of people and bags on the boat which meant that we were all sat on a hot bobbing boat for about forty minutes, not fun.

We finally set off and the journey seemed to take much longer than the trip over, also the sea was a lot choppier and there were a few people not feeling well, I was one of them but managed to hold it together. I am definitely getting better at boat and bus travel!

Back on dry land we got some food and then found a shuttle bus to take us to Bali airport, this was fine but instead of being a bus to the airport it had three destinations and I don’t think any of us on the bus realised this till the driver got on and asked us where we were going!

Luckily we were the second drop but most of our journey seemed to be along a stretch of road which was being resurfaced.

Anyway we arrived at the airport and as we were about 12 hours early we got a taxi driver to take us to a nearby hotel which wasn’t going to cost us a fortune. The one we ended up with was nice and had air conditioning (a must after our sauna room in Gili) and a pool which we made the most of before going out to find some western comfort food to ease our full on day of travel,

Mmmmm Pizza Hut.
