**Friday 26th**

Left to our own devices but with a few pointers on what is worth seeing, we headed out on a long circuit to see the waterfront, [Pike Place Market](http://www.pikeplacemarket.org/), the first ever Starbucks, and a [few](http://www.playitagainsports.com/) [outdoor](http://www.snowboardconnection.com/) [stores](http://www.rei.com/) to drool over [snowboarding gear](http://lib-tech.com/snow/snowboards/skate-banana/). Once back at our temporary home, we prepared the boards for [Crystal Mountain](http://www.skicrystal.com/) as it was going to be an early wake up to get fresh powder.

Jon had put the word around at work that we were here and organised a few beers with some work friends so we went for a gentle night out knowing we had a 5.30am start the next day. Rolling in at 1.30am after drinking far too much Jack Daniels, we set the alarms and crashed out hoping we would feel fine in four hours.

**Saturday 27th**

Up on time, we loaded the car and hit the road, stopping for gas, coffee and [Jack in the Box](http://www.jackinthebox.com/) before winding our way up through some amazing landscape to the resort. We got there just before the lifts opened so had chance to get a good place in line.

It had dumped overnight and we were treated to the deepest powder we had ever boarded in. It was the perfect bluebird day (bright blue skies and fresh powder) and an amazing treat having not been on snow since April 2009. We warmed up on the freshies at the edges of some groomed runs, then Jon took us off in to powder bowls in between the runs where it was waist deep in places.

We stopped off for lunch early at 11am to avoid the crowds and get back out just as everyone else was stopping for theirs. It worked perfectly and we were rarely on a run with more than two or three others. Feeling suitably worn out, we headed back to the car and set off home, looking forward to an evening of watching TV and chilling out.

**Sunday 28th**

Following a decent nights sleep and a bit of a lay in, we grabbed breakfast and Jon offered to show us some of the sights. We checked out the [Seattle Space Needle](http://www.spaceneedle.com/) and then met up with Jon's girlfriend Jenna and her friends at a bar, to catch the final third of the men's Olympic ice hockey final. As we got there Canada were leading USA 2-1 with 15 minutes to go. The atmosphere was great with a fair few Canadians there as well. With a minute to go USA pulled off their goaltender and added another outfield player in an effort to grab an equaliser. With 20 seconds to go they did and the bar erupted. Canada went on to win the game in sudden death overtime, and we left our new found American friends thinking about what could have been.

From the game, we headed to Fremont to see the [Fremont Troll](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont_Troll) and [Gas Works Park](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_Works_Park) followed by a stop at [Umi's](http://www.umisakehouse.com) for happy hour sushi and another trip to Snowboard Connection to drop off Jon's board and check out jackets.

We spent the rest of the evening working out how to get from Seattle to Whistler on Tuesday, eating spaghetti bolognese and drinking red wine. All in all another perfect evening.

**Monday 1st**

After getting a rough idea of how to travel from Seattle to Whistler with Amtrak, we decided to head down to the main station and buy our tickets there, so we could speak to a real person. We borrowed Jon and Jenna's scooter which made getting around much easier, and saved a whole load of time, having got to the station and realised we didn't have our passports. We rode home and grabbed them and headed back in to town to get everything sorted.

Nat spent some time the previous evening looking for travel yoga mats and we found a place uptown that looked promising. Another few minutes on the scooter and we were in [Barefoot Yoga](http://www.barefootyoga.com/), which was actually a mail order outlet and had no shop front to handle walk-in customers. We managed to get one for cash though and headed back to Pike Place Market for some food, before returning to Jon's to get ready for a night out.

This is where it starts to go a little wrong. We received an email from our chalet host explaining they have been trying to contact us by phone for a while but had no luck. They had been speaking to us on email for a few days so not sure why they waited so long to tell us they couldn't call.

There is a problem with our accommodation in Whistler, and we are trying to sort it out at the moment. We leave tomorrow morning at 7.40am so we are trying to fix it all before we set off.

I wonder where we will end up staying...

While it is being sorted by the company we booked through, we decided to head out with another friend in Seattle, Ron, for some food and a walk around some other sights. We went to a little French restaurant for a food happy hour, and walked around 1st Avenue to see some of the older side of Seattle. We missed a chance to [tour the underground](http://www.undergroundtour.com/) of Seattle, but are planning to catch it on the way back through.
