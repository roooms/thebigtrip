---
title: "Grand Canyon"
date: 2010-05-05 18:17:00
author: "Natalie"
---
Our journey to the Grand Canyon took us out of Vegas, past the famous sign, around Lake Mead then over the huge Hoover Dam which is famous not only for being a Dam but also for having been in many films including the start of Goldeneye. We actually went over it three times to get pictures of the Welcome To Nevada and Arizona signs!

The rest of the journey was travelling through more huge expanses of land and mountains but as we climbed higher we could feel the air temperature was dropping and by the time we got to the Grand Canyon it was feeling much chillier than Vegas!

The Canyon is huge and has amazing markings from all the different rock and minerals that have formed it over millions of years. The sun was on its way down while we were there and it was great to see the colours of the rock subtly change as the light changed.

We finished our day with a drive back to Williams which was our room for the night, time for a good nights sleep ready for our mammoth seven hour drive back to Santa Clarita, Six Flags.
