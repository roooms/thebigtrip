After realising I had lost the phone we got some helpful staff to radio the bus drivers to look for it on the bus, meanwhile I tried to forget about it and enjoy our day at probably the worlds most famous Zoo.

We bypassed the huge queue for the first Elephant feeding and headed to the Crocoseum to get good seats for the show.

The show was a chance to see different animals in action, we had birds flying overhead, a guy who went for a swim with a giant Snake, the Elephants made an appearance and of course the famous Croc show was the finale. It was a bit sad to watch a clip of Steve Irwin on the big screen talking about his work but being at the Zoo you really felt like all the people there were keeping his ideas and dreams alive. It made me want to stay and become a Wildlife Warrior!

Next stop was the Kangaroo walk through where we got to feed some Roo’s and Wallabies, they were pretty lazy and obviously knew the score when people came up to them with food!

The Zoo is pretty big and we managed to get around nearly all of it, the only bit we had to miss out was the Animal Hospital. We were able to stroke a sleepy Koala and snap some pics of a little Joey, we also saw a baby Wombat who with his Mummy joined in for a Wombat picnic!

There was a Komodo Dragon which I was excited to see as we missed out on going to see them in the wild, also plenty of snakes and of course loads of Crocs.

I think the best bit of the day was the afternoon Elephant feeding, this session was much quieter and we got to feed all three of the gorgeous Indian Elephants whilst learning a lot about them.

At the end of our day we went back to check on any phone updates but they had had no luck, we left our details and just had to keep our fingers crossed.

We got on our Greyhound bus which took us to Brisbane, there we checked in to a hostel and went to the bar to drown our sorrows with a couple of beers.

The next morning we were up and out early to enjoy a bit of sunny Brissy before getting our bus to Byron.

We walked down to the South Bank where there was a Saturday morning market, lots of café’s and a man made lagoon with real sandy beach along the river edge. We also walked past the Brisbane version of the London Eye!

We sat and enjoyed a bit of brekkie before walking over one of the many bridges and back to the hostel to collect our bags and get our bus to Byron Bay.
