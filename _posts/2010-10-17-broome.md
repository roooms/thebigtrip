---
title: "Broome"
date: 2010-10-17 21:53:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
Most of the group from the tour had all organised to stay at Cable Beach Backpackers in Broome, so on our arrival we piled in to the reception to begin the mass check-in. This coincided painfully with Happy Hour, and only one person working both the bar and reception. Once we were all checked-in we joined the existing clientele in a few cheap drinks. We had loads of booze left from the tour on the ‘wombat’ (tour bus) but weren’t allowed to drink it in the backpackers as it was licensed. After a few beers we grabbed the esky from the bus and headed in the direction of the beach, stopping for spirits at the bottle shop on the way. Needless to say, it got messy. I ventured barefoot in to the bush in search of firewood with our tour guide Chris, only to hot-step it back out again after hearing about the Coastal Taipan. We had a nice controlled fire and ended up in the sea, before a quick visit from the Police and local Rangers asking us to put the fire out.

The next few days settled in to a very comfortable pattern of dragging ourselves from bed to the poolside and lazing around until an hour or so before sunset. Then we would head off to the beach with a slowly dwindling supply of booze to watch the sunset and go for a swim. I think everyone was enjoying the lack of a plan having spent the previous ten days following a very fun, but tight schedule. We did make it down to the beach during the day a couple of times whilst in Broome, but with the heat hitting a dry high thirties and no easy shade, most of us couldn’t handle it for long.

On our second to last day we walked down to the beach just before sunset and joined a camel ride up Cable Beach. Our trusty steed was named Bully and we were closely followed by the once proud owner of the title ‘Fastest Camel in Australia’, Randy, who was permanently rubbing his nose against my leg.

We ventured in to the centre of Broome twice; once to try Mango Beer and Chango (chilli and mango) Beer at Matso’s Micro-Brewery before ending up at Murphy’s drinking Guinness, and our second visit was to Roebucks where there was a wet t-shirt competition and lots of Jack Daniel’s. Classy. We finished our last night with a pizza and three hours of sleep before the alarm woke us for the journey to the airport. Next stop Darwin!
