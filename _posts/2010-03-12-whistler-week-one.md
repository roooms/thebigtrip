---
title: "Whistler week one!"
date: 2010-03-12 22:47:00
author: "Natalie"
---
Ok so wow what a week!

Since we last posted we have settled into our new chalet along with a rapidly increasing group of boarders and skiers oh and one who hasn't decided yet! They are mainly from good ole blighty and one from Bournemouth! A few aussies and a kiwi and a German guy, it's a good combo.

We went to a house party with the group which was crazy, there were taxi's waiting outside like a club to take people home when they were done. It's a good crowd, we've done some communal meals and been out on the slopes together. Oh and the hot tub and sauna are awsome!

Our first couple of days were spent checking out the slopes, we had a mixed bag of conditions and achievements. I had to buy some new boots as I was not getting on with my Vans, so now have some beautiful Burton boots, I'll get a pic on before they get wrecked!

Since then we've had a perfect powder day with blue sky and some untouched powder, we found the best waffles in Whistler at the Crystal Hut on Blackcomb and also saw the paralympic torch arrive up the mountain! We took a day off as it was rather a warm spring day and spent time wandering the village and checking out the continuing set up for the paralympics.

Then we were back to the slopes. We built a kicker with some of our housemates, Toby, Will and James and spent the afternoon trying out jumps, I've included some pics. Dan managed some 180's and I managed to get over the jump a few times which I was happy with as that was my first attempt. We also found some really friendly birds called whiskeyjack's who are happy to come and sit on your hand or ski pole just to see if you happen to have any food, we did treat them with some bits of our sarnies to keep them happy! We also bought some tickets for the men and women's super combined paralympic event next tuesday so that should be pretty cool.

Yesterday we had a day of snow which was great but it also meant it was not good visibility, we did a few runs sometimes we could see and sometimes we couldn't! There was lots of powder though which made it worth it. I tried out some switch riding and Dan was continuing to practice his jumps. When we came down to the village we found the Olympic mascot's chilling in some chairs so I had a little sit down with them. Then we came home and chipped in to cook an awsome meal.

Today we woke up early to a winter wonderland, the snow had made it down to the village and it looked very exciting outside. We got up got sorted and were at the lift by 9am! Found plenty of fresh lines through the powder even though it was pretty busy and did a bit of winding through some trees. After burning our legs out we rode down to the village and rewarded ourselves with a starbucks. We looked in a dome/tent that was set up to show the history of the paralympics which made us feel really excited about watching the super combined next week!

It is still snowing fairly heavily now so will be another early night tonight ready for a full day tomorrow!
