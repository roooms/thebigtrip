---
title: "Byron Bay"
date: 2010-11-18 20:17:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
We got to Byron Bay before sunset and made our way to the Holiday Village where we had booked our first night. The layout reminded us of Cable Beach Backpackers in Broome with a single storey set of rooms surrounding a swimming pool and eating area. The guys running the hostel gave us a great welcome before we dumped our stuff in our room and headed out to see the market before it shut. We had a good walk around the main street and all the excitement of our previous visit came flooding back.

We picked up some food and back at the hostel bumped in to our two new roommates Ivan and Jen. They were from Brisbane and had decided to stay on another night as they were having so much fun. After food we soon got caught up in a game of Ring Of Fire with Jen, Ivan and some other Brisbanites and two Norwegians, Trude and Live. They were all hitting the booze hard, and at 10.30pm when the hostel closed the outside areas to quiet things down, we headed over the road to Cheeky Monkeys to continue the fun. Suitably drunk we ended up dancing on tables until we were thrown out around 3am.

The next morning we gathered last nights group together and went for breakfast at a little café called Bohemia. Between us we managed to piece together most of what happened the night before. Ivan and Jen were heading back to Brisbane but some of the others we met were here for the rest of the day so we grabbed free surfboards from the hostel and made our way down to the main beach. The lack of surf was a bit of a blow, but we topped up the tan for a few hours and then made our way back to have some food.

With all our Brisbane friends back at their day jobs, Monday started quietly. Then we met Kayla, a Canadian planning on spending some time in Australia, finding a job eventually, but not before enjoying herself in Byron Bay for a few days first. The weather wasn’t great though so we hung around the hostel most of the day and the guys running the place told us there would be a Goon Pong competition that evening. After missing out on the one in Mooloolaba we knew we had to join in this time. We picked up a cheap box of goon from the bottle shop and settled in for a second night of drunken fun. We beat Kayla and her team-mate Solaug (nicknamed Soup as we couldn’t get the pronunciation right) but lost out in the next round. That evening we met a crazy group of Swedes who would soon also all have nicknames courtesy of Kayla; Slutty Swede, Speedy Swede, Sneaky Swede and Sloppy Swede.

The following morning was harder than normal. It turns out cheap goon removes large chunks of your memory. We headed down to the beach and spent the day soaking up more sun. That evening, feeling slightly better, we ventured across the road to Cheeky Monkeys for food then took a walk back in to the heart of Byron to claim the free drinks we were entitled to, thanks to numerous coloured wrist bands we had collected from promotions on the beach that day. Kayla also tried her first ever pie (an Aussie staple food) and nearly fainted at how good it was.

Another day of sunshine and we were off to the beach again. Nearly everyone from the hostel made it down for the day. We played volleyball, tried skim-boarding and occasionally jumped in the sea to cool off. I also took a walk from the beach through the bush to a road where we hired a house during our 2006 visit to Byron. We topped off the day with pizza and drinking games. The Circle Of Drunk was born. I vowed to spend the whole of the next day drinking as it was going to be our last in Byron. The Swedes were very keen to join in.

Thursday was here and after a quick bomb of the swimming pool, I cracked open my first beer and joined the breakfast gathering at the benches in the hostel. We spent the morning there as it was cloudy and nobody wanted to venture out. The hostel had a basketball court, so in between sips of my beer, I tried (and usually failed) to shoot hoops with whoever had the energy. Nat enjoyed a lay-in but as the weather improved we decided it was our last opportunity to do the lighthouse walk.

The peninsula at Byron Bay creates a few great surfing beaches, and has a beautiful lighthouse at its point. The walk took a couple of hours, and I got to see just how good the surf was at some of the other beaches around Byron. By the time we got back to the hostel it was food time and Kayla and Slutty Swede (who turned out to be an accomplished chef) decided to cook a ‘family’ meal for us all. Slightly drunk by now, they foolishly left me in charge of collecting payment from everyone. I think I got most of it. We had a great last gathering around the tables before we had to say our goodbyes. One of the guys running the hostel offered to drive us up to the Greyhound bus stop, and seven or eight of our new friends piled in to the shuttle to come and say goodbye. We had a thirteen hour bus ride ahead of us all the way to Sydney. It was going to be a bit painful, especially knowing the guys would all be back at the hostel enjoying themselves, but we had a Halloween themed date with some close friends planned and were looking forward to seeing sunny Sydney again. It would obviously be sunny right?
