Our ferry left the dock soon after we had made it aboard and we grabbed a milo as a sweet treat to pass the time on the short hop over to the island. I had a quick scout around for Mark and Sam but they were not on the ferry.

The journey was only about half an hour and the sun was just going down as we docked, we got off the ferry and were just checking out the taxi’s when we heard our names being shouted. It took me a couple of seconds to realise it was Sam and a bearded Mark! It was strange and fabulous to see them and then completely normal after about ten minutes!

It turned out that the place we were planning on staying cost a small fortune to get to and the guys had been waiting for us for about two hours, we decided to go to a closer area called Lonely Beach and got in a taxi. Chatted all the way there about our crazy day’s of travel and also about their trip so far.

Once in Lonely beach we had to walk around for a while to find a place which was in our budget, it was pretty busy but we finally found a place for 400 baht which would do for the night. Got changed and went for some much needed food and beer. Found a place that was in between a street stall and a restaurant and parked ourselves there for yummy Thai food and a few cheap Chang’s. We were kept entertained by the owners very cute little boy and exchanged stories of our travels.

The next morning we were up early to go and find another room as we were informed by the owner that the price was going up that day for peak, peak season meaning Christmas and would be 700b per night!

We walked along to the next small village but found nothing, had breakfast before going back and looking again around Lonely beach. We were beginning to worry as we headed back to Magic Garden to check out, with our backpacks on in the midday sun we didn’t want to be walking around for long. We hadn’t gone far when someone said for us to ask in the 7/11. The guy in there took us behind the shop where there were little bungalows with bathrooms, cold water showers but for only 300b we couldn’t complain so we snapped two of them up. Feeling relieved we headed to the beach to relax for the afternoon, after watching the sunset we headed back for our cold showers followed by food and beers at the same place as the night before.

Xmas Eve

We spent the day sunbathing, swimming, reading up on India and generally chilling out which made a change to my usual manic Xmas eve at work!

That evening we went to another local food place and started our crimbo celebrations with some more tasty food and of course some beers followed by a rotti (type of pancake). Next we went to a bar near to where we were staying and we soon found out was home to a few Katoey’s (lady-boys). There was free pool and we could choose the music and at one point in the evening we used our own ipod to get some different xmas music on rather than listening to Mariah over and over again! Dan got behind the bar to make up some G'n'T's but unfortunately we still had to pay for them! We had a really fun night and at about 3.30am after settling our tab we walked down to the beach to go for a Xmas dip in the sea. On our way back to our bungalows me and Dan decided to grab a quick snack before falling into bed at about 5am!

Merry Christmas!

Christmas Day

Surprisingly we were not up very early but when we did rise we put on our tacky xmas hats and did our secret Santa presents as well as a couple of gifts from Fiona, cheers Fifi! We all ended up with chocolate which we had pointed out to each other in the shop the previous day and tucked into a bit of it before heading down to a place we had seen called Joy’s cottage for breakfast. We treated ourselves to eggs and bacon and were amazed to have freshly baked bread rolls too.

After a bit of a chill out back in our rooms we got a taxi to Bang Bao which is a little fishing village home to some good seafood restaurants. We had a little wander around all the little stalls, me and Sam treated ourselves to a ring each before walking down to the pier and checking out the lighthouse.

As the sun was setting we found ourselves a good looking place to eat and ordered some tasty Thai food for our Christmas dinner with a difference.

After our heavy one the night before we only had one drink with dinner and decided to leave it at that and head back to Lonely beach, after all doing a bit of a skypathon to catch up with the families back home we spent the evening together in one room chatting, listening to the ipod and snacking on what would become our new favourite cheap eat, crisp sarnie’s.

What a great Christmas day, so glad we got to spend it with Mark and Sam!

Boxing Day

After a day off of the beach we decided to head back for another day of relaxing. This time we walked down to the far end which was much more beautiful and we had it to ourselves. Stayed there the whole day and watched another lovely sunset before wandering back to get ready for our evening of tasty food, a couple of drinks and a delicious treat known as a roti, a kind of pancake. We had ours with chocolate and vanilla sauce and they were, to quote Mark‘s new favourite word, phenomenal!

We picked up some cheap beers and headed back to the room to drink and play cards.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2011/01/19557210-DSC04104.JPG)

We had had another beach day and were feeling like getting around the island especially to see what Long beach, our original destination, was like. After managing to find two bikes to rent from two different shops and eating our overpriced mediocre breakfast we headed down to a shop to buy fruit and stuff for crisp sarnies. Whilst in the shop I also managed to accidentally drop some make-up on the floor which I had to pay 100b towards. I got a milo to cheer myself up and had to be glad they hadn’t charged me the full 180b. The worst thing was it just slipped out of it’s card packaging something which also happened to me with a bottle of beer falling out of it’s cooling jacket the other night, that time though it didn’t break and we got a free beer!

We set off on our adventure and after turning back from a waterfall which wanted to charge us 200b each to enter we found another which was much quieter for only 20b. The only downside to this one was that it was down a bumpy dirt track. We arrived in four pieces and after extracting a bee sting from Marks foot we set off for the waterfall. After a ten minute walk we arrived at one of the pools and got in for a dip, there was a rope swing to play on and it was even deep enough for Dan and Mark to jump into. We walked back down to the bikes and set off again but it wasn’t long before we had to stop. Mark and Sam’s bike had a flat back tyre, we managed to get it pumped up and carried on but it didn’t look good so we swapped bikes and Dan, being more experienced took on the dodgy one. Along the road I could feel that the tyre was getting flatter by the minute and luckily we came across a garage. The guy in there pumped the tyre up and wanted to fix it for 250b, we thought we would be fine just stopping every now and again to fill it up but almost as soon as we set off again we realised that it wasn’t going to work. Then Mark’s bike ran out of fuel, Dan went off on his own to get the tyre fixed while we walked to find some gasoline. Luckily we came across someone who siphoned some out of their own bike for us, then it was time to really experience Thai culture by getting three of us on the bike! We managed it fine and soon we were back at the garage waiting with Dan while a guy resembling a laughing Buddha fixed the tyre. Hindsight is a wonderful thing and I think we should have taken the events of the morning as a sign and maybe taken the bikes back but we were so far round the island now we decided to press onto Long beach, we topped up the fuel and were on our way, again!

Our final bit of journey seemed to take forever, a lot of that was to do with the final bit of road or should I say motocross track. We couldn’t believe there was a resort at the end of the crazy, winding, bumpy, steep road that we were attempting to ride along but finally we arrived at Tree house lodge on Long beach. We wandered round to the beach which was really quiet and sat down to our extremely late lunch whilst taking in the lovely view and sunset. We left before it got too dark and all felt happy that we weren’t staying there. Not enough Katoey’s around for us!

The journey back started off ok but soon as we were heading up a steep hill our bike started spluttering and we ran out of gas, we couldn’t believe it. Mark and Sam went back down the hill to a garage. While we waited for them two people stopped to help, one even phoning his mate who turned up with gas for us. They spoke little English and it was really hard to explain that we had help already. Luckily Mark and Sam were not long and soon we were on our way again.

It was gone 8pm by the time we rode back into Lonely Beach and we were all slightly chilly with extremely sore arses! After getting no money back for the tyre we decided to drown our sorrows of the day so that we could laugh about them. We went to our local (the lady-boy bar) where we ended up having a very messy night which consisted mainly of Singa beer and Jagerbombs and resulted in Sam and me dancing on the bar with one of our new friends and later on falling asleep in our clothes.

Good times!

Oh we also made a pact to climb Mt Everest together before 30 or 35 for me and Dan and as the pool table had Everest written on it then it must be a sign, uh oh!

The next couple of days were spent recovering from our big night out, chilling on the beach and helping each other plan routes in our respective countries.

New Years Eve

This day started out as many of the others had with a bit of beach action, we stayed down there though to watch the sun go down on 2010.

Back at our little huts we managed to sink a few Singa’s together and got so carried away chatting that we suddenly realised it was nearly 10pm! We quickly got ready and went to the local for some drinks, got a free round of Jagerbombs from the boss which was about time really considering the amount of baht we had dropped in there.

Didn’t have time for food and before we knew it we were getting sprayed with snow spray and silly string and being piled into the back of a truck and ferried down to the beach party.

I think we arrived just in time and celebrated with each other as the clock struck midnight, we watched some awesome fireworks and got a bit emotional when they played Big Ben chiming and Auld Lang Syne. Dan went for another dip before we walked back to town to find some food. Just had time to go to the Dalmation internet cafe to skype home and wish the families Happy New Year!

New Years Day was another chilled one. We gave the beach a miss and just relaxed around the huts before going out for munchies in the evening.

Then it was suddenly our last full day in Koh Chang. We spent it at the beach, where else! And had a nice evening with Mark and Sam enjoying some Thai curries with beer then back to the room for a few games of cards.

On our final day we got up early and were joined for breakfast by Mark and Sam, they saw us onto the taxi and after numerous goodbyes we were whisked off to the ferry port. We are going to really miss them and have had the best time but will look forward to catching up with their blog as they continue their travels.

When we arrived at the port there was a ferry waiting so we all piled on and soon were off to the mainland to continue our journey to Bangkok.
