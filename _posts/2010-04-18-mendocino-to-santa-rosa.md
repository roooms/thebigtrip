---
title: "Mendocino to Santa Rosa"
date: 2010-04-18 23:30:00
author: "Natalie"
---
We made a few stops along highway 1 on our way to Mendocino to take in the amazing coastline, we took some pics and carried on to our destination.

Arriving in Mendocino felt automatically calming, it only consists of a few streets so it wasn't long till we found the LP recommended Seagull Inn. This was a bit pricier than the book had suggested but was the cheapest in town, especially on a friday night and included a room delivered organic breakfast. Oh well we need a bit of pampering, sometimes! The room was really sweet, old fashioned and cosy. We headed out to have a walk along the headlands and beach before the sun set completely. The town is full of art galleries, jewelry shops and bookstores. It's obviously a wealthy little place.

We ended up eating in a cafe/restaurant next to our Inn at a place called the Mendocino Cafe. The prices were reasonable and the food was outstanding, this set us up for, well not much really. Both feeling exhausted from all the walking and driving we settled in our comfy room for the evening and looked forward to breakfast.

The next day we were up early and the sun was breaking through warming up the day. Breakfast was as good as all the people had described in the room's guest book!

We got sorted and got out of our room, ready for the big drive down to Bodega Bay which is just north of San Francisco.

We had another look around Mendocino as we had just been told at checkout that good ole Murder She Wrote was filmed here. It didn't really jog any memories from watching it but would be cool to see it now and be able to recognize the place!

So we set off for Bodega Bay, we had a few scheduled stops planned along the way to take in some more sights but about ten minutes after we set off we had an unscheduled stop in the form of a flat rear tyre, bugger!

Pulled over, got the tyre changed and phoned Budget who were not as helpful as the sat nav which showed us where the nearest Budget was to get a new tyre.

So new plan now was to head to inland Santa Rosa, stay there the night and sort the tyre out in the morning, sorted.

On with our day, at a slightly slower pace we stopped at Manchester State Park Beach and took some pics of the fantastic wind swept beach, also saw a snake!

Then next was the Point Arena lighthouse, one of the only lighthouses on the west coast that you can go up.

We saw some Seals basking on rocks below and could see the San Andreas Fault line.

We trucked on to Santa Rosa and after declining a nights stay in a smoking room of one Motel, yuk! We came across our friend the Travelodge who could put us up for the night and provide us with breakfast, bargin! We didn't see much of this area but everyone was friendly enough. We headed down the street to a local bar/restaurant for some food and a beer and were entertained by drunk locals playing pool. All in all a great night out!

Off to San Francisco tomorrow once the tyre is sorted, I'm looking forward to it. We have got three nights there so should be just enough time to see all we want to see.
