---
title: "San Luis Obispo & Santa Barbara"
date: 2010-05-02 00:53:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
From San Simeon we continued down the coast to San Luis Obispo. We parked up and had a walk around what looked to be the main shopping street. Everyone seemed effortlessly cool, in a surfing/skateboarding kind of way. The majority of the shops were focused on one or the other and along with the blue sky and mid-twenty's heat, it made us want to be on the beach.

We were planning on staying the night in Santa Barbara though, so had to get back on the road. An hour later we were driving down State Street, the main 'strip' in Santa Barbara, looking for a cheap place to spend the night. Prices varied a lot but in the end we got a cheap hotel only two blocks from the beach. It was right next to a train crossing, had shared bathrooms, and a leaky tap in the small sink in our room. But we had ear plugs!

We dropped off our bags and went for a walk along the beach. There was a fantastic pier with three or four shops and restaurants on it, and we sat at the end for a while watching pelicans cruise past and sea lions breaking the surface of the water. There was an awesome sunset and we were kicking ourselves for forgetting the camera. We would have time in the morning to get some pictures though.

We walked back up State Street to find Soujourner Cafe as recommended by Lonely Planet, and got some great food before wandering back to the hotel for some more trip planning.

In the morning we woke up to a few clouds in the sky and walked down to the pier again to get the pictures we missed the night before. There was no perfect sunset but there were still a few pelicans and sea lions making it worth the walk. There were a few homeless people making great sand sculptures for donations on the beach. We gave one a few dollars to get a picture of him with his soldier sculpture, before going for breakfast at Cajun Kitchen where there was a twenty minute wait due to its popularity.

With a limited amount of time left in the USA we had decided to drive through Death Valley to see the spring flowers, go to Las Vegas for two nights, then the Grand Canyon, followed by a long drive back to Santa Clarita to go to Six Flags Magic Mountain Theme Park. It was going to be a lot of driving, but it would be worth it. Leaving Santa Barbara we headed for Barstow, a little place just south of the loop through Death Valley we would be driving the next day.
