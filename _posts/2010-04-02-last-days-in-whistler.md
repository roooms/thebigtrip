---
title: "Last days in Whistler"
date: 2010-04-02 11:55:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
Our last two days in Whistler were powder filled and incredible fun. With lots of our house mates having headed home already, or moved in to staff accommodation now they had jobs, we found ourselves getting to bed a bit earlier and heading up the mountain just after first lifts, at around 8.20am.

In those last few days we also met more of the folks staying at other Seasonaires chalets which in turn meant we boarded some new and exciting terrain and drank and ate at new bars and restaurants. This left us feeling like after a whole month we were still just scratching the surface of what Whistler had to offer, and we really considered staying a bit longer.

On our final day the snow had stopped and it was sunny and hot, perfect weather to... thoroughly clean our room and book our bus to Vancouver. It was an emotional goodbye as we left our new friends and headed to the bus loop ready for our two hour journey south. The sunshine stayed with us on our way to Vancouver and we got to enjoy the incredible scenery that we'd missed on the rainy, grey journey to Whistler a month ago.
