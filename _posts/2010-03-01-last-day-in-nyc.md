On our last day in NYC we woke up to snow!!

Had a pretty chilled morning and packed up, went to Soho for a last look around and made a last minute purchase as by now my feet were bored of getting wet in my Uggs! At first the snow didn't settle but by the time we got to Central Park in the afternoon it looked like a winter wonderland! Had a little wander before going back to the hotel and getting our car to the airport. On the way the snow was getting heavier and looking like we may not fly however luckily the US are much better at dealing with it than the UK and had a huge fleet of snowploughs clearing the runways and we got to watch our plane's wings get de-iced which was pretty cool.

Not a bad flight and arrived at Seattle early, Jon picked us up and we had a chilled night with a few drinks, looking forward to checking out Seattle!
