---
title: "Sydney, Australia"
date: 2010-08-06 02:26:00
author: "Dan Brown"
---
Checking in at Christchurch Airport we found we were not on the 10:15 flight to Sydney. After spending twenty minutes talking to the customer service desk it was established that when Virgin changed the date of our flight from Delhi to London they had cancelled and re-booked all the remaining flights on our ticket; but missing out the New Zealand to Australia leg. Clearly not our fault, we had to wait patiently while two people from Air New Zealand rang around to see if they could recover payment to get us back on the flight in time for its departure. It was all sorted with ten minutes before boarding began although we were not in the seats we expected. The flight was otherwise easygoing. The weather was looking good for our arrival in Sydney.

We landed a bit ahead of schedule and the arrivals area of the airport was almost empty; a stark contrast to our last visit in 2006 when we had to queue for ages to get through passport control and customs. We grabbed our bags - just one each now making it a much more comfortable walk - and got some Aussie dollars from an ATM before jumping on the underground rail network to Hyde Park.

We were meeting Fiona who works in the city for lunch and she took us to the very popular, [Din Tai Fung](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Din_Tai_Fung) for their ‘world famous’ dumplings and a few celebratory drinks. The food was fantastic and feeling suitably full we went with Fi to have a look around her office on the 51st floor of the Ernst & Young building. The views of the city were great from that high up. From the city centre we caught the train to Kings Cross where we would be staying with Natalie’s cousin Sammy and her partner Giles. Our first weekend would turn in to one big party as Nat celebrated her birthday on the 25th.

On the Friday night we went to a birthday party at a restaurant called [Omerta](http://www.omerta.com.au/) for one of Fiona's work friends. On the Saturday we went to Paddington where we spent the first hour in a bar opposite the market stalls drinking cider. We did manage a look around the market before wandering home and getting ready for a meal at a Vietnamese restaurant called [Xage](http://www.xage.com.au/) in Surry Hills. We checked out a few bars afterwards, but didn't have a really late one as we were going for a Sunday roast the next day at [The Local Taphouse](http://www.thelocal.com.au/).

Sunday was Nat's birthday and Fi had organised an awesome roast at The Local Taphouse and Nat's friends from home had sent presents over for opening. It was a great day and we stayed in the pub until the early evening. A weekend of alcoholism was complete and a week of recovery could begin.

Most weekday evenings were spent chilling with Sammy and Giles, cooking some good food and watching films. Nat found a nice dress in Ripcurl whilst wandering around [The Rocks](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rocks,_New_South_Wales) which she bought with some birthday cash. Wednesday stood out in particular because it rained almost all day. We spent it inside but ventured out to the cinema to see Inception with some free tickets Fiona got for us. It was a great evening with a fantastic film.

A few more quiet evenings (with a steady supply of wine) and we were ready for our '[Christmas in July](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christmas_in_july)' trip to the Blue Mountains!
