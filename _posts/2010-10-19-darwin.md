title: "Darwin"
date: 2010-10-19 04:00:00
author: "Natalie"
---
Our flight to Darwin was a struggle for me as I was feeling pretty rubbish from our late night, it was worth it though. I think!

We were soon in a shuttle bus on our way to our hostel with Dee and Clo who were to be our roomies for the next few nights.

Our first day ended up being a bit of a quiet one. We got some food and checked out the pools on the roof of the hostel then I spent most of the rest of the day in bed getting over the previous night.

Day two was much more productive. We walked down to the esplanade and along to a man made lagoon and wave pool, these pools were created as the sea is off limits due to the jelly fish and crocs!

By now the heat was getting to us so we took refuge in the air conditioned library before walking back to the hostel.

It was Dee’s birthday so we joined the girls with some of their mates on the rooftop bar, we sank a few jugs of happy hour beer, got pizza to share and surprised Dee with a cake, sparklers and party poppers. After a game of ring of fire the girls were off to sample some of the other bars but we decided to hit the sack.

The next day we lounged by the pool for a few hours, well actually we were in the pool for most of the time as it was too hot to be completely exposed to the sun.

Later on we got down to Mindil Beach to check out the markets and watch the sunset. There were lots of food, jewellery and clothing stalls, we finally decided what we wanted to eat and took it to the beach to munch while we watched a gorgeous sunset. Next we watched some after dark entertainment in the form of a fire dancer, he was really good but just as he finished a few huge drops of rain and some gusts of wind were all the warning we had of the torrential downpour that followed. We took cover under a big parasol next to one of the stalls but we soon realised that it wasn’t going to blow over and walked to the huge taxi queue.

By now we were laughing at how soaked we were. The queue was never ending but then our luck changed and a mini bus turned up that was going to town and it seemed that all the people in front of us were going somewhere else so we jumped in.

Back at the hostel it was only a few hours till we would be getting our taxi to the airport so time to decide weather to sleep or just stay up. I just hoped that the rain would stop for our flight to Cairns.
