Our second week in Sydney started with a day of organising and Christmas food shopping. We were heading off to a house in the Blue Mountains for a weekend of Xmas in July fun and games. We managed to cram our borrowed jeep with food, drink, bedding and clothes then Fi, Sammy and I squeezed in the back while the boys, Dan and Giles had the luxury of the front

Once we had fought through the end of day traffic we had a good run to the house and arrived just before Daniel and Stuart. We spent the evening drinking mulled wine, eating very non festive pizza and salad and catching up.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/08/13998313-IMG_4822.JPG)

The next day was our Christmas Day and Santa had left us all gifts outside our doors. We had a joint effort in the kitchen prepping all the food for the day then Jo and James arrived and the party started.

We had lots more mulled wine and made mince pies while listening to Xmas carols.

We had a feast which was amazing, lots of wine, fortune cookies and party poppers followed by secret santa pressies and a lethal game of bingo with shots! Then we decided it would be a good idea to run around so we had two games of musical chairs followed by a much more sedate game of Cranium.

We had some cheese and biscuits and went to bed feeling thoroughly full, well what else would you expect on Christmas Day!

Our Boxing Day was very chilled out and we sat around chatting after breakfast until we had to leave, took lots of pics outside the lovely house, Wombat.

Had quite a productive rest of the week, on Monday we found a wallet and phone which we took to the Police. Tuesday was a planning day and rewarded ourselves with ‘tight arse Tuesday’ food in Wooloomooloo with Sammy and Giles.

Wednesday was great fun as we helped Fi move into her new pad, we spent the whole afternoon lifting and carrying boxes and furniture so treated ourselves to some treats from a lovely supermarket called Thomas Dux and had a picnic on the carpet.

Thursday was mainly spent on the phone but by the end I had sorted out our main flights for the rest of the trip.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/08/13998356-IMG_4944.JPG)

Our third week was a bit more chilled and started with a pie from the famous Harry’s Café de Wheels in Wooloomooloo. We walked to Circular Quay to take a look at the Bridge and Opera House then walked onwards to Darling Harbour via a cute little market at The Rocks, sat and soaked up some sun at Darling Harbour, I did a bit of people watching and Dan had a little sleep.

We went over to Fi’s place that night and moved our stuff into her’s for a few nights. We went out for food just up the road from her pad.

I had one of my favourite days in Sydney the following day, we got up and went for brunch at a fab place called Tiger Mottle then me and Dan went to Paddington Market and Oxford St for a wander and a bit of retail therapy. Walked back to Fi’s and we went to Watson’s Bay on the bus to have fish and chips over the bay. Had a couple of vino’s before heading back to Fi’s place.

Sunday we went to cheer on Jo and James in the annual 14K city to surf run, it was a gorgeous day to be at Bondi and it was packed. The guys did really well and while they went home to shower Fi, Dan and I did the Bondi to Coogee coastal walk.

We got back to Bondi and met the guys for celebratory drinks and a very tasty nibble at a place called Sabbaba.

Spent the next couple of days doing planning and getting some more stuff for the next bit of our trip. On Wednesday we moved back to Sammy and Giles’s and then went to meet my friend Hannah in Bondi, she brought her children Duke and Zsa Zsa and we had a lovely afternoon lunching and playing in the park, the kids not us!

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/08/13998418-IMG_5015.JPG)

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/08/13998415-IMG_5014.JPG)

Our last full day in Sydney we spent over in Manly, once we got back Fi, Jo, Daniel and Stuart came to Sammy and Giles’s and we all went over the road to have a farewell dinner at a yummy Japanese place.

Said goodbye to everyone for now but we are looking forward to coming back in a few months, I think we have really enjoyed being here as we haven’t felt like we needed to race around and see the sights and also it has been so nice to stay with and be around people we know.
