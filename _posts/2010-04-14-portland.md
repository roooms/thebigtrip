We arrived at a still sunny Portland and found ourselves a good ole Travelodge for the night.

Got a tip off from the friendly guy Chris who checked us in, to head to an area called the Pearl District.

We ended up driving around what we thought was the Pearl District for about half an hour to find somewhere to park and eat.

Didn't have a late one so we were fresh for the next day.

Started our day by booking our next two nights accommodation, one in a different area of Portland and the other in a place called Shady Cove which is going to be our base for seeing Crater Lake.

We walked back to where we were last night after realising that we had been Downtown and hadn't even found the Pearl District! We stumbled across a square which had a group of local Cirque du Soleil performers who were putting on mini shows to advertise that the new show has just openend in Portland! As I have always wanted to see a show we chatted about it but decided to try to see it in Vegas instead.

We sat and had a coffee in the square though and watched some amazing performances.

Dan spotted a couple of Freebord riders and chatted to them for a while about the bords and had a go on one. After sitting in the sun for a while we walked to the Pearl District which was a really nice area with cool shops, nice looking bars and resturants. Dan got a much needed pair of flip flops, we carried on down to the waterfront and when we came back into the city we arrived at Voodoo Doughnut.

An extremely well known shop which is open 24 hours a day and pretty much always has a queue outside, which we joined to get a fix!

We only had one each although it would have been easy to have more. The logo is pretty apt for my name too!

Next we headed back to the car and drove to the Hawthorne District to find our room for the night at the Bluebird Guesthouse.

This was a cute house/hostel with communal areas, laundry machines available, breakfast and various bedrooms, some with bathrooms others with shared bathrooms.

Our room was really comfy and we shared a bathroom. Headed out to check out the area and we found lots of residential streets of gorgeous wooden houses all with balconys and blossom trees and gardens full of flowers. Then we came across a street with lots of shops, a couple of bars and resturants. It had a really hippy vibe, we stopped and had some food and a drink and just missed the rain.

Walked back to the Bluebird and planned our route to Shady Cove over a hot chocolate, I know so rock and roll!
