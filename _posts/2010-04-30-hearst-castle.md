We went to visit a stately home called Hearst Castle. It is situated high up on a hill surrounded by thousands of acres of rolling hills and overlooking the beach.

The huge house was part designed and owned by a guy called William Randolph Hearst who was a buisness man and carried out a dream he had as a child to build a property on his familys land.

He was inspired by trips to Europe and you can see this all through the main house and guest cottages. He also had an actual Castle in Wales! He basically wanted it to be a lavish place to entertain his famous friends where they could swim in his gold plated pool, play tennis on their own court and even visit unusual animals in his own private zoo. But guests were only allowed two drinks as he couldn't stand drunk people!

There are still Zebra's that roam the land from when Hearst introduced them!

He had collected hundreds of antiques and artifacts over the years for which he built the house to accomodate. Some Egyptian statues which were about 3000 years old.

This was a beautiful, fantasy place where I could totally imagine hanging out and having an amazing party!

The pictures will bring it all to life.
