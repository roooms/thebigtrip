At 7am we started our two day journey to Luang Prabang. We had some breakfast then were taken down to the border crossing which is split up across a river, on the Thai side we had our passports stamped out then took a small long tail boat across to Laos where we filled in all our forms and got our Laos visa’s. This was quite a long process but we got there in the end. Then it was more waiting around so we stocked up on snacks for the journey and a cushion each as the boats are not meant to be too comfy!

Finally we got called to get into a tuk tuk which took us down to where the boats were leaving from, we thought this was it but then we were told we had to wait another hour till we could get on the boat We sat with our new found friends and discussed the stories we had heard about this two day boat trip along the Mekong, some good and some bad they all contained stories of little space and we all decided that we would revolt and get another boat if ours was getting too full. Just as me and Dan were tucking into a sandwich we were told to go and get on the boat.

As we boarded ours we noticed that there were some comfy looking car seats but they had already been taken so we were left with the bench seats, good job we bought the cushions! The boats are really quite nice, all made of wood, open sided so there is loads of air and thankfully ours probably only had about 70 people on board so we were happy.

On the boat we sat with our new Canadian and French-Canadian friends Vanessa, Sarah and Michelle. We could also hear most of what Ben (from the bus the night before) was saying and he came over to chat to us once or twice in his increasingly drunken state, good job he was funny although the French girls didn’t think so and I thought he was going to end up overboard a couple of times!

The journey was really relaxing and a much better way to travel than by bus, our day was taken up with chatting, eating and watching the gorgeous view of lush forest and hills, small villages by the water and the occasional Water Buffalo.

We arrived in Pak Beng at about 5pm and were greeted off the boat by a few people advertising their accommodation, we took a girl up on her offer and walked up through the tiny one lane village to our beds for the night. After dumping our stuff and choosing our breakfast we went for a very short wander and chose one of the small restaurants for some beer Lao and some local food. We all went for Buffalo in different styles and it was very tasty, the beer Lao went down well too and after a complimentary shot of local lao lao whisky (bleugh) we went to another place for a couple more beers before walking across the street and collapsing on our rather hard bed!

The next morning we had our breakfast and collected our packed lunch from our host and we were off down to the boat which actually left early. The day was spent doing more of the same but this time we had more stops along the way to pick up and drop off people and things such as bags of rice and baskets of ducks and chickens which went on the roof We even picked some girls up from one of the smaller boats which came up alongside us.

After 8 hours we finally arrived at Luang Prabang and we were glad to be off the boat and walking around even with our backpacks on. We went with the girls to a guesthouse that we had been given a card for and all managed to get rooms at a good price. After settling in we went out together to get food and drinks to celebrate our arrival. We sat in a gorgeous garden lit up by loads of fairy lights and ate some scrummy food, the beer wasn’t bad either and even the free shot of lao lao whisky was flavoured. Next stop was another bar where a guy was playing guitar quite well so we settled in and sampled a few buy one get one free lao lao cocktail such as lao colada, laotini, laorita and the stinger! Thankfully the closing time for all bars is 11.30pm so that prevented us from drinking too many more and we headed home to bed.

Unfortunately the next day I wasn’t feeling too good and no not a hangover, I would have preferred that! Managed to have a bit of breakfast then went back for a sleep. Had a walk around the old quarter in the afternoon which has lots of French colonial buildings but it wasn’t long before I had to go back to the room and lay down again. Didn’t go out again and just tried to sleep it off.

The day after I felt a bit better so managed some food and went up to Phu Si which is a temple 100m up a hill in the middle of the city. The views were amazing, there were a lot of Buddha statues including ones to represent days of the week. Whichever day you were born on you have a Buddha to bring you luck. There was also a Buddha footprint which was much bigger than I thought it would be.

After our hike up the hill we went to chill out for the afternoon at a place called Utopia which overlooked the river and had comfy day beds to lay out on.

Ended our evening with a wander around the night market.

Luckily I was feeling much better today, we went to a place called Saffron which had been recommended to us again by Katie. Both of us had egg, bacon and cheese bagels which were amazing. Next we went off to find a tuk tuk to take us to a waterfall called Kuang Si, had to wait around for a bit but finally got in one with three other people and set off on our hour long journey.

The waterfall is in a public park which also houses rescued Sun Bears, these Bears are kept by poachers in small cages and their bile is drained from them to be used in medicines. Luckily there is a lot being done here to stop this from happening and it was great to see some of these gorgeous bears happily lounging around.

We walked or rather climbed up to the top of the waterfall which gave a great view over the forest then came back down and got into one of the aqua pools for a refreshing dip, there were also some little fish nibbling at my toes when I stood still. Dan didn’t stand still long enough, he had had enough of that in KL!

There was also a rope swing which was fun and Dan was of course the one to start the trend of jumping in from one of the smaller falls. Time to go so we headed back to get our tuk tuk home.

That evening we went back to the night market for food which was really tasty and picked up some cake for our journey the next day.

We got up at 5am today to go and watch the Monks receive their Alms, on the way we got persuaded to buy some rice and pork wrapped in banana leaves to give to the Monks as they came past. We sat and waited for the Monks to come and felt a bit silly but as they walked past and we were putting the food into their ornate urns we felt glad to be a part of their morning ritual.

It was getting light by now and after our offering was over we walked down to a different street where we saw a lot more Monks walking the streets and collecting Alms, it was a great sight to see and we took a few pics but there were so many tourists there getting really close and taking pictures of the Monks. It kind of cheapened the whole experience and we decided it had been nicer earlier when we had been among a few people to watch them.

We went back to our guesthouse after grabbing some breakfast and played with the little girl at the house whilst waiting for our minibus to take us to Vang Vieng, no doubt it would be working on Laos time.
