title: "Los Angeles"
date: 2010-05-05 18:55:00
author: "Natalie"
---
Arriving in LA was really exciting, we drove straight to Santa Monica and had a wander along the beach-front and saw the Pier. We wanted to stay in this area but unfortunately so did lots of other people and so the hostel we went to look at was fully booked for the next few nights. So we ended up staying in a area called Culver City which was kind of in the middle of everywhere we wanted to go. We checked into yet another funny little motel called the Half Moon and got some food at a very quiet Italian Restaurant across the road, not bad.

So the thing about LA is that it is really spread out and you have to drive to get anywhere! On our first full day here we went to Griffith's Park and drove up to the Observatory which gave us really good views of the Hollywood sign. We also went into the Observatory which was free entry (bargain) and housed lots of cool stuff to do with space including a 75 year old telescope which you can see Venus and Saturn through when it isn't so windy! We decided to leave and go back another night to look through it.

Next to go and see was the Avenue of Stars, we happened to park right next to Natalie Cole (so close) and saw the likes of famous stars such as, John Lennon, Marlin Brando, Neil Armstrong and perhaps best of all Lassie!

The next day we drove to Venice Beach and checked out the body building area, not many bodybuilders there which was a shame :-)

Watched the heated basketball games going on and a guy who looked like he could work for Cirque du Soleil doing his stretches and standing on top of a very high bar above the rings!

Next we drove along to Newport Beach to check out a wave called 'the edge'. It gets to 30ft in the summer but is right on the beach so tends to slam body-boarders onto the sand, nice.

We continued down to Laguna beach which was a really nice area, lots of money around but also had a really chilled out feeling.

We then took a big drive back to the Observatory to try to look through the Telescope. Unfortunately we arrived a bit late and the queue was already fairly big to get into the Telescope so we didn't get to see through it. We did see an Iridium flare which is a satellite that is moving and being lit up by the already set sun, it was only visible for about 6 seconds and was nice to see something. We got some pics of LA all lit up too which was cool.

For our last day in LA we went to Malibu beach, it was a nice warm day a bit breezy but we sat and watched surfers and some paddle boarders on the gentle waves. Also saw lots of Pelicans chilling out in their own little Lagoon.

After a chilled out morning we went to Rodeo Drive and looked at all the designer shops and fabulous houses.

Our final stop was a trip back to Santa Monica, we walked down onto the pier and took a few last pictures before driving to the airport.
