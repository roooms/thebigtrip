---
title: "Vancouver"
date: 2010-04-08 12:05:00
author: "Natalie"
---
After a great journey from Whistler along an amazing coastline and aided by the film Cloudy With a Chance of Meatballs, we arrived in Vancouver. Got a cab to our hotel which was basic but comfy and right in the middle of things. Then we headed out for some food. We had looked up a resturant called Coast which looked good for seafood and it was! We shared Calamari then had Fish and Chips, I know it sounds a bit boring but it was done really well and we had seen lots of it going out so that was that!

Our first full day in Vancouver started with a yummy and very filling breakfast then we walked to Stanley Park which is on the water and spent the afternoon at Vancouver Aquarium. It was a brilliant place with all the usuals but the stars of the show were the White Sided Pacific Dolphins and the beautiful Beluga Whales (they are grey when born then turn white when they reach adulthood). We watched both of their shows which were only about ten minutes each and were nice as they were more about information rather than getting the animals to do too many tricks. They did do some though which included the Beluga's splashing the kids at the front of the seating, ha ha!

We also watched an episode of Planet Earth in 4D, this was a 3D program with added extras like getting sprayed with water as if getting splashed with spray from Dolphins jumping through waves, blown with wind as we looked down on the shallow seas and poked in the back from Sea Snakes! Very cool, much better than I expected.

We walked back to the hotel via the Olympic rings, did a bit of planning for Vancouver Island then went to watch Alice in Wonderland in 3D. I thought it was fab and am getting quite used to wearing the glasses!

The next day was a bit lazy but mainly because it was extremely windy and rainy. Did some catching up with e-mails then left to go and get some more info for Vancouver Island.

We walked through an area called Gastown which has a clock powered by steam, then through Chinatown and a Chinese Garden.

Next on the list was English Bay which has another Inukshuk and some beaches which I'm sure look good when the sun is out! We went to start walking through the other side of Stanley Park to where we had been the other day but were stopped by the Poice saying that some trees had been blown down in the park and it wasn't safe.

Instead of going back to the hotel we went to get a water taxi over to Granville Island which is kind of the industrial area of Vancouver, on the way we saw a couple of trees that had been totally blown out of the ground. On Granville Island we walked through a really cool food market then the weather got worse so we headed for shelter inside a place called The Sandbar, had an awesome tapas selection and some yummy red wine to help me warm up! After heading back over to the mainland we got some Easter chocolate treats from the Rocky Mountain chocolate shop and I pretty much ate all of mine as soon as we got back to the hotel!

We then decided after watching the news that Vancouver Island was off the list. It had been hit hard by the storm that we had and most of the Islands power was cut. New plan was to head down to Seattle then to Yellowstone.

Last day in Vancouver and we woke up to some sun, yey! Headed over to north Vancouver via water bus and went to see the Capilano suspension bridge. It was good but not really worth the money. Came back over to the mainland and went back to Stanley park, hired some bikes and rode around it. On the way we saw a Black Squirrel, a Heron and a Raccoon! Saw the start of a nice sunset then got some food and an early night ready for our early start in the morning and journey back to Seattle.
