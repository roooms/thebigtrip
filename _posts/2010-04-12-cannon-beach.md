Left Seattle mid afternoon in our shiny white Toyota Corolla, felt good to be on the road on the start of our proper road trip.

Once we were away from the city traffic we headed down the coastal route which took longer but was much more interesting. We got amazing views and drove over incredible bridges, all the time trying to beat the sunset to Cannon Beach.

We almost made it but missed it by having to come inland for the road into our destination.

Cannon Beach looked fab though and we got a third time lucky on accommodation, balancing price, location and breakfast!

Dumped the bags and headed to a place called the Driftwood Inn for some scrummy late food. Hoping for good weather for the next day we headed back to our cute room to research our surroundings.

Woke up to a lovely sunny day, had fruit for brekkie and headed down the street to a place called The Sleepy Monk for a coffee on a recommendation from our hotelier. Then took a walk down to and along the beach, past a huge rock called The Haystack which is the main attraction and home to Puffin's when they come to land to lay and hatch their eggs.

We kept walking till we reached the rocky end to this beach, it was a long way but worth it for the views and pictures looking back along Cannon Beach.

This is such a peaceful, rustic yet energetic place, lots of dogs on the beach and as we walked back more and more people turning up to spend a warm spring day on the beach. It was also nice that it wasn't built up, it seemed to be local people and businesses that have been here for years. Hopefully it'll stay like this. Definitely one of my favorite beaches!

We got some sandwich's, had a little picnic on the beach and lay down for a bit before tearing ourselves away to make the drive to Portland before dark.
