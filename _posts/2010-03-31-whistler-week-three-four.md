---
title: "Whistler week three & four"
date: 2010-03-31 10:13:00
author: "Natalie"
---
This week has gone by so quickly we had to actually work out what we did each day!

Sunday we went on a free avalanche awareness course with a group of boarders led by a skier and learnt how to use transceivers to find people buried in the snow, then how to use probes and the correct way to dig people out. Then we dug a huge snow pit and created columns of snow to look at the different layers created by ice and fresh snow falling on top of it and then did a pat test to see if the snow was easily dislodged. It was pretty cool then we boarded back down through some fresh powder which we had found.

We had a really good visibility day on Monday so took the oppurtunity to go up to the peak on Whistler mountain which is the highest point on the mountain. There were amazing views and we managed to get some pictures of the Innukshuk (which is a symbol that means "someone was here" or "you are on the right path" and were used by the first nations to find their way through the land). We spent the rest of the day in the park practising our jumps and also practising switch which is coming along ok.

Tuesday was our fresh tracks day when we were on the lift up to the mountain at 7.15am!! It was all worth it as we were treated to fantastic views as the sun came up over the mountains and lit up the freshly groomed piste. We had a great breakfast then we were off to make our fresh tracks. We were all having a great day till James decided to ski into a snowy ditch, he twisted his knee so we called it a day as the visibility was low anyway (hence the crash) and headed back home. Then we decided to go tubing which was really fun.

That evening most of the house went out to see Electric Six. On the way there after a couple of wines I managed to sprain my ankle again!! We got to the bar and instead of dancing I was icing my ankle, still had a good night though and Electric Six sounded great!

Had a couple of days off, one to rest the ankle and do some planning for the next bit of the trip and the other to go dog sledding. We had an awsome day, started by meeting the eight dogs who would be pulling our sled, we helped to harness them up then we got into the sled and we were off. The dogs were really exited especially Miso who was a dog in training and started by just running alongside the sled and after about ten minutes of getting in the way he got harnessed in and had to work! We both got to have a go at standing on the back of the sled and then when we got back to the cabin we held a two and a half month old puppy called Franky. Very tempting to take him home!

After that we had a little wander round the village and then went to an animal shelter called WAG. It's a great shelter where while the dogs are there waiting to be re-homed anyone can go in and walk a dog for free. We walked a dog called Levi, he was gorgeous, very energetic and loved trying to pick up very long sticks. Had a really nice walk with him along a river. When we got back we had a little look around the puppy pen and the cattery.

The next day we were back on the slopes, Dan demo'd a different snowboard and we headed to the park. After a couple of runs James had another crash, this time it was off a jump and he landed on his face, ouch! We all took a break and James decided to go down the mountain, on the way he got worse and we ended up taking him to the mountain patrol office where they assessed him and gave him oxygen. They arranged for him to go to the the medical center once down the mountain. We took him there and he ended up having to stay in for obs for a few hours as he had concussion and a suspected fractured nose.

We went back to get him a bit later and he was much better, just felt really hungover.

For our last weekend in Whistler we had Ron and his girlfriend Cheryl up from Seattle. They arrived Saturday and we had a sunny afternoon on the slopes then we came back, got ready and went to a resturant called Elements. It is a modern tapas resturant and the food was really yummy! The guys were pretty knackered from getting up early and driving up to Whistler so we called it a night ready to get up early for more action on the slopes.

The next morning we all headed up Whistler mountain for a couple of warm up runs then took the peak to peak to Blackcomb, the plan was to go to the Crystal Hut for amazing waffles but Cheryl fell and twisted her knee so her and Ron headed back down to the village and me and Dan went for waffles. We did a few more runs then went down to the village for a coffee with Ron and Cheryl before seing them off and also to see James off on his bus.

That night we went out to a bar in the village called GLC (stands for Garabaldi Lift Co not Goldy lookin chain!) with some of our house mates to see a couple of guys called the Hairfarmers. They are a coverband and are really good, I think there might have been more people there than at Electric Six!

When we left the snow that was predicted was falling as low as the village so we went home to try to get as much sleep as possible ready for a powder day!
