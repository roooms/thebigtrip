We finally left Wanaka after having to get our faulty gas bottle changed, yet another problem to get fixed! We took a shorter route to Queenstown which took us over a mountain range and gave us some fab views.

We arrived in the sun but it felt much colder and there were signs of a really heavy frost, it looked like snow! Got a site at the campsite we had stayed at before and took a walk into town and to AJ Hackett. After a bit more deliberation we booked ourselves up for the Nevis Arc and Nevis Bungy for the next day. We went back to the van to celebrate our booking with a cup of tea, as we got back Kerry and Dean who we had been hanging out with in Wanaka turned up at our van and we decided to go for some drinks.

It was really nice walking into the the Buffalo Bar, they had a cosy looking fire going, monkey nut shells on the floor and the best bit was that nearly all drinks were $3 each! Needless to say we ended up having quite a few each which then brought on extreme hunger and we knew just the place to cure it.

Fergburger is the busiest burger place I have ever seen, no matter what time you walk past it there is a queue. We had been told about it by so many people and read about it in the Lonely Planet, we really hoped it lived up to expectations.

The menu was vast and it was a job to decide what to have, well it was for me anyway. Dan had already decided he was going for the biggest one aptly named Big Al, two burgers, two eggs, beetroot, all the salad, cheese, relish, aioli! I went for Cock Cajun which was a cajun chicken burger with aioli, salad 'n' relish. They totally lived up to our expectations (and not just because we were a bit tipsy!) really really tasty! As usual I couldn't finish the fries I had so desperately wanted so I carried them back to the van just in case!

Woke up early the next day despite the previous night out, threw the fries out and got the brekkie on, then we were out the door to get to AJ Hacket and off to do our extreme activities. When we arrived Dan was asked if he wouldn't mind going 45 mins later as they needed to change the bungy, he said that was fine so they changed me too and we ended up getting free pictures and video worth $160! We had just enough time to treat ourselves to a Starbucks and use the free internet on the lovely mac's at the AJ Hackett place before being called outside to get on the bus.

![](/thebigtrip/assets/images/2010/07/11973617-P1050132.JPG)

It was a forty minute bus ride to get to the canyon, as we drove up the last steep part of the hill we could see the Arc platform sitting high above the river below. I could feel my legs go slightly jelly-like.

Inside the building we got signed in and weighed then it was off to the Arc for me to go first, as we walked across the suspended bridge we could see the Bungy platform which was also looking exremely high!

We got to the Arc platform and instantly someone was dropped, we watched them fall then fly over to the other side of the canyon. It looked like a long way!

A few more people went and then it was my turn, I got harnessed up then after checking with the guy in charge Dan taped the Go Pro video camera to my hand. I chose to go forwards and got attatched to the swing, then I was winched out from the platform, asked if I wanted a countdown to which I replied no and before I knew it I was off dropping through the air then swooping forward towards the other side of the canyon covering a total of 1000ft with the river flowing below me. It was awesome and I instantly wanted to do it again! I got winched back up and over to the platform and thanked the guys. As a second swing was only $35 I decided to do it after Dan's Bungy.

We both had to get harnessed up for the Bungy for safety even though I wasn't jumping, then we took a little moving cage over to the suspended jump pod. There was a high amount of anxious energy in the pod, mainly from the two girls who were jumping. Dan was really calm and after they all had their legs strapped up he was the first to be taken through to the chair to be attatched to the bungy. I strapped the Go Pro to his hand and got some pics of the bungy prep, then he was up and doing the bungy shuffle to get to the edge of the ledge. I started videoing then the guys said 3 2 1 and he was off, no hesitation just a little look down and a nice dive of 134m! He looked so chuffed afterwards, it was over quick but so worth it.

Back in the hut we were checking out our pics and video and I noticed that I didn't have a video of my swing so I asked one of the guys, he told me that the video hadn't worked and I could do it again if I wanted! Amazing, first we got free pics and vid and now I was getting a free swing!

We went back over to the swing platform and the same guy was still operating it, I got harnessed up and the Go Pro strapped to my hand again then was talked into going upside- down and backwards off the platform. I had to wrap my legs around the straps and lean my head right back which was a bit nerve wrecking then I got dropped by surprise again (best way) and watched the river coming towards me then the sky appear.I got myself the right way up and listened to the air rushing past my ears and took in the awesome views of where I was hanging. It was great doing it a second time, I took in a lot more of the experience because I knew what to expect.

Unfortunately my video worked that time, it looked really cool Dan got a Bungy t-shirt and I got an Arc cap plus our certificates and cd's with pics and vids on. We got chatting to some other brits whilst waiting for the bus back to town and they invited us to go for a Ferburger with them, we decided it'd be rude not to and spent the whole journey thinking about what to have.

By the time we got there we were starving but knew what to order, I had a Codfather which was a blue cod fillet, beer battered in a bun with dill tartare, salad, relish, aioli and Dan went for a Sweet Bambi which was a venison burger with the usual stuff plus a plum sauce. They were fab mine was even better than the first one and Dan's was super tasty!

Feeling rather full we went back to the site to re-live our experiences and to plan a route out of extreme Queenstown.
